CAR PHOTOS
==========
Current photos are free-licence (CC BY-SA 4.0) images from Wikimedia Commons, downloaded by tools/commons_fetch.py.
Each photographer is credited on the car page and under "Photo credits" in the footer (data in site/credits.json).
They are illustrative and amateur-quality; replace them with your own showroom photography when you have it.

To replace a photo: save your image here as BM-101.jpg ... BM-118.jpg (ids are in site/data.js) and delete that car's entry
in site/credits.json (or the whole file once every photo is yours) so no wrong credit is shown.

  hero.jpg   wide home-page image (currently the BMW Welt in Munich, CC BY-SA 4.0). Delete it and the hero becomes text-only.

Tips: landscape 3:2 (e.g. 1500x1000), JPG under ~400 KB.
